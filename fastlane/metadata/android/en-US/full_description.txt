Iskra is a small animated cat-girl who lives on your phone's screen. Tap her to talk, drag her anywhere, or just let her wander, nap and peek out from behind the edges.

<b>She helps right away — no sign-up, no internet:</b>
• alarms and timers: “set a timer for 10 minutes”
• reminders that survive a restart: “remind me tomorrow at 9 to call mom”
• notes: “note: buy milk and bread”
• the weather in your city: “do I need an umbrella?”
• the flashlight, volume, apps, settings, web search
• “Surprise me” — tiny games with the world around you

<b>A real conversation when you want one.</b> Connect a free “brain” with one tap (OpenRouter: sign in with Google or e-mail, nothing to copy) or use your own key from DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude or any OpenAI-compatible server. Replies arrive sentence by sentence and can be spoken with natural cloud voices or the free system voice. Say “Iskra” or “Spark” and she listens — recognized right on the phone.

<b>Made for everyone:</b>
• English and Russian, following your phone's language
• Share any text to Iskra, or select it and tap “Ask Iskra”: explain, summarize, translate, save as a note or a reminder
• Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts
• Android 8 to 16, phones, tablets and foldables; step-by-step tips for Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, Honor, OPPO, OnePlus, vivo, Tecno and others so the system doesn't close her
• TalkBack, large fonts, light and dark theme, calm motion
• Battery modes; she asks for the fast refresh rate only while moving

<b>Private by design:</b>
• memory, notes and the chat stay on your phone; delete anything at any time
• API keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore
• listening for her name works fully offline; audio never leaves the phone
• no ads, no trackers, no accounts
• daily cloud limits and prices, so you never overspend
• free and open source (GNU GPL v3)

Iskra is an AI program, not a person — and she says so if you ask.
